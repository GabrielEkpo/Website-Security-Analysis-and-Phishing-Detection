Common phishing indicators

Suspicious URLs

  • Typographical or lookalike domains
  • IP addresses instead of registered domains
  • Very long or confusing URL paths
  • Unusual subdomain structures

Urgent language

  • “Verify your account”
  • “Confirm your password”
  • “Unusual activity detected”
  • “Click here immediately”

Security signals

  • HTTP instead of HTTPS
  • Weak or missing transport security
  • Requests for credentials or payment details
  • Suspicious sign-in or verification forms

Typical targets

  • Banking and financial services
  • Email and social platforms
  • Payments and online commerce
  • Cloud account portals
